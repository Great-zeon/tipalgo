MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION

The Three Pillars of the SportyMill Engine

01

Bivariate Poisson Simulation

Football is a low-scoring sport that conforms strictly to Poisson point processes. SportyMill calculates independent scoring parameters λhome and λaway using form-weighted offensive and defensive ratings. We then run 10,000 Monte Carlo match simulations to establish true joint probability score matrices from 0-0 through 7-5.

P(X = k) = (λk × e-λ) / k!
02

Expected Value (+EV) Filtering

A bet is only qualified when the model's calculated win probability multiplied by the decimal odds exceeds 1.0 after removing the bookmaker's margin. This guarantees that over large sample sizes of hundreds of selections, the law of large numbers operates in your favor rather than the bookmaker's.

EV = (Model_Probability × Bookmaker_Odds) - 1.0
03

Fractional Kelly Bankroll Engine

Even with a proven mathematical edge, bad bankroll management leads to ruin. SportyMill implements the Kelly Criterion dampened to a conservative 0.25x fraction. This mathematically sizes each wager in proportion to its edge, optimizing geometric growth while capping peak drawdown swings.

f* = 0.25 × [(b × p - q) / b]
