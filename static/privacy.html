Regulatory & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Version 2.4 | SportyMill (TipAlgo Platform)

1. Introduction & Commitment

SportyMill ("we", "us", or "our") operates the TipAlgo quantitative sports analytics platform. We are dedicated to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of all personal and device information entrusted to us by our subscribers and users.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you access our web application, create an account, purchase subscription plans, or run match expectancy calculations, and describes how we protect your information in compliance with international data privacy standards including the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and applicable global regulations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information essential to providing verified quantitative intelligence and safeguarding your account:

  • Account Credentials: Username, registered telephone number, and securely hashed passwords (stored via multi-round bcrypt with salted hashing). We never store raw passwords.
  • Hardware & Session Identifiers: Anonymous browser fingerprint hashes, operating system platform, and UUID device identifiers utilized strictly for the 2-device concurrency enforcement policy.
  • Billing & Transaction Metadata: Payment reference tokens, payment timestamps, and subscription tier validity generated via Paystack. We do not store credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank PINs on our servers.
  • Analytical Usage: Filter selections, simulated betting slip configurations, and screener queries to optimize model speed and cache frequently queried fixture markets.

3. How Your Information Is Used

We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data, phone number, or bet configurations to third-party marketing brokers or bookmakers.

Your information is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Authenticating authorized user sessions and delivering SMS One-Time Passwords (OTP) for account recovery.
  • Enforcing concurrent hardware session limits to prevent credential theft and unauthorized account sharing.
  • Calculating Kelly criterion staking recommendations calibrated to your personal bankroll risk profile.
  • Delivering critical platform updates, fixture model refreshes, and subscription notifications.

4. Payment Security & Third-Party Processors

All payment transactions are handled through Paystack Payments Limited, a certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processor. All communication between our platform and Paystack occurs across 256-bit SSL encrypted channels. We retain only the cryptographically verified transaction reference and activation duration to unlock Pro access.

5. Data Retention & Erasure Rights

You maintain full authority over your data. You may request complete deletion of your account profile, registered phone number, saved bet slips, and device authorization records at any time by contacting our data protection desk at support@sportymill.com. Requests are resolved within 72 hours.
